# Reported speech básico e gerúndio vs. infinitivo

Nesta lição você vai aprender a **relatar** o que alguém disse ({{en:She said she was busy.}}), perguntou ({{en:He asked if I could help.}}) ou mandou fazer ({{en:The boss told us to finish by five.}}), com atenção à diferença entre **say** e **tell**. Na segunda parte, vai ver qual forma usar depois de um verbo: **-ing** ({{en:I enjoy working here.}}) ou **to + verbo** ({{en:I want to leave early.}}), incluindo os casos em que a escolha muda o sentido ({{en:stop to smoke}} vs. {{en:stop smoking}}).

## Reported speech: a ideia

Quando repetimos as palavras de alguém **depois**, o inglês "recua" um tempo verbal, porque o momento da fala original já passou. Isso se chama **backshift**.

| Fala original (direct speech) | Relato (reported speech) |
|---|---|
| present simple → past simple | {{en:"I work here."}} → {{en:She said she worked there.}} |
| present continuous → past continuous | {{en:"I'm leaving."}} → {{en:He said he was leaving.}} |
| past simple → past perfect | {{en:"I sent it."}} → {{en:She said she had sent it.}} |
| present perfect → past perfect | {{en:"I've finished."}} → {{en:He said he had finished.}} |
| will → would | {{en:"I'll call you."}} → {{en:She said she would call me.}} |
| can → could | {{en:"I can help."}} → {{en:He said he could help.}} |
| must → had to | {{en:"You must sign."}} → {{en:She said I had to sign.}} |

Além dos verbos, **pronomes** e **expressões de tempo e lugar** mudam de ponto de vista: *I* → *he/she*; *my* → *his/her*; *here* → *there*; *now* → *then*; *today* → *that day*; *tomorrow* → *the next day*; *yesterday* → *the day before*; *this* → *that*.

{{en:"I will send the report tomorrow," said Ana.}} → {{en:Ana said she would send the report the next day.}}

> **Dica:** se o que a pessoa disse **ainda é verdade** ou acabou de ser dito, o backshift é opcional: {{en:He said he lives in Recife.}} (e ainda mora). Em exames, porém, o recuo é a resposta esperada.

## Say vs. tell

Esta é uma das confusões mais comuns.

| | Estrutura | Exemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **say** | say (that) + frase — **sem** pessoa, ou *say to* + pessoa | {{en:She said (that) she was tired.}} / {{en:She said to me that she was tired.}} |
| **tell** | tell + **pessoa** + (that) + frase | {{en:She told me (that) she was tired.}} |

Regra prática: **tell precisa de alguém; say não**. ~~She said me~~ e ~~She told that~~ estão errados. *That* é opcional em ambos.

Expressões fixas com *tell*: *tell the truth, tell a lie, tell a story, tell the time, tell the difference*. Com *say*: *say hello, say goodbye, say sorry, say yes/no*.

## Relatando perguntas

Perguntas relatadas **não têm inversão nem ponto de interrogação**; a ordem vira a de uma afirmação.

**Perguntas com palavra interrogativa** (*what, where, when, why, how*):

{{en:"Where do you work?"}} → {{en:He asked me where I worked.}} (não ~~where did I work~~)

{{en:"What time does the meeting start?"}} → {{en:She asked what time the meeting started.}}

**Perguntas sim/não**: use **if** ou **whether**:

{{en:"Are you coming to the party?"}} → {{en:She asked if I was coming to the party.}}

{{en:"Have you paid the invoice?"}} → {{en:He asked whether I had paid the invoice.}}

Verbos úteis: *ask, want to know, wonder* ({{en:I wonder if he got my message.}}).

## Relatando ordens, pedidos e conselhos

Estrutura: **tell / ask / advise / warn + pessoa + to + verbo**. Negativa: **not to + verbo**.

{{en:"Finish the report by Friday."}} → {{en:The manager told us to finish the report by Friday.}}

{{en:"Please send me a copy."}} → {{en:She asked me to send her a copy.}}

{{en:"Don't forget the deadline."}} → {{en:He reminded me not to forget the deadline.}}

{{en:"You should check the numbers again."}} → {{en:She advised me to check the numbers again.}}

Note que aqui não há *that*: ~~told us that finish~~ está errado.

## Gerúndio vs. infinitivo depois de verbos

Em inglês, quando um verbo é seguido de outro verbo, o segundo vai para **-ing** ou para **to + verbo**, dependendo do primeiro. Não há lógica perfeita; é questão de memorizar grupos.

### Verbos + -ing

*enjoy, finish, avoid, mind, suggest, consider, keep (on), practice, miss, can't stand, can't help, give up, don't mind, feel like*

{{en:I enjoy working with numbers.}} — Gosto de trabalhar com números.

{{en:She suggested postponing the meeting.}} — Ela sugeriu adiar a reunião.

{{en:Would you mind sending it again?}} — Você se importaria de enviar de novo?

{{en:We should avoid making the same mistake.}} — Devemos evitar cometer o mesmo erro.

Também depois de **preposições**: {{en:I'm interested in learning more.}} / {{en:Thanks for helping me.}} / {{en:He left without saying goodbye.}}

### Verbos + to + verbo

*want, need, decide, hope, plan, promise, agree, refuse, manage, afford, learn, offer, seem, expect, would like, would love*

{{en:I want to improve my English.}} — Quero melhorar meu inglês.

{{en:They decided to hire a consultant.}} — Decidiram contratar um consultor.

{{en:We can't afford to lose this client.}} — Não podemos nos dar ao luxo de perder esse cliente.

{{en:She managed to finish on time.}} — Ela conseguiu terminar a tempo.

### Verbos que aceitam os dois, sem mudar o sentido

*like, love, hate, prefer, start, begin, continue*

{{en:I like working here.}} = {{en:I like to work here.}} (No inglês britânico, *like + -ing* é mais comum para prazer.)

### Verbos que mudam de sentido

| Verbo | + -ing | + to |
|---|---|---|
| **stop** | parar de fazer: {{en:He stopped smoking.}} (largou o cigarro) | parar para fazer: {{en:He stopped to smoke.}} (parou o que fazia para fumar) |
| **remember** | lembrar de algo que fez: {{en:I remember locking the door.}} | lembrar de fazer: {{en:Remember to lock the door.}} |
| **forget** | esquecer algo que fez: {{en:I'll never forget meeting her.}} | esquecer de fazer: {{en:Don't forget to send it.}} |
| **try** | experimentar, testar: {{en:Try restarting the computer.}} | esforçar-se: {{en:I tried to call you, but it didn't work.}} |

## Texto: relatando uma reunião

> {{en:Yesterday I had a meeting with our new client, Mr. Lima. He said that his company was growing fast and that he needed help with payroll. He told me he had used three different accountants in the past two years and that none of them had answered his emails quickly. He asked me how many clients we had and whether we worked with international suppliers. I said we did, and I promised to send him a proposal by Thursday.}}
>
> {{en:He also wanted to know if we could start the following month. I told him that we could, but I advised him to keep his current accountant until the transition was complete. He agreed to do that. At the end, he asked me not to share the information with anyone, and I assured him that everything would stay confidential. I enjoyed talking to him; he seems to be the kind of client who doesn't mind paying for good service. I'm looking forward to working with him.}}

## Erros comuns de brasileiros

1. ~~She said me that...~~ → {{en:She told me that...}} ou {{en:She said that...}}
2. ~~He told that he was late.~~ → {{en:He said that he was late.}} / {{en:He told me that he was late.}}
3. ~~She asked where did I live.~~ → {{en:She asked where I lived.}}
4. ~~He asked me that I help him.~~ → {{en:He asked me to help him.}}
5. ~~I enjoy to work here.~~ → {{en:I enjoy working here.}}
6. ~~I want working from home.~~ → {{en:I want to work from home.}}
7. ~~I stopped to smoke last year.~~ (significa que parou para fumar) → {{en:I stopped smoking last year.}}

## Na prática

Relatar conversas é rotina: resumir para o chefe o que o cliente disse (*He said he needed the report by Friday and asked us to send a quote*), registrar um pedido (*She asked me to check the figures*) ou repassar uma recomendação (*The auditor advised us to keep the receipts*). Em e-mails, -ing e *to* aparecem em fórmulas fixas: *I look forward to meeting you*, *We would like to confirm...*

## Resumo

- Reported speech: recue um tempo (*present → past, past → past perfect, will → would, can → could*) e ajuste pronomes e marcadores (*tomorrow → the next day*).
- **Tell** precisa de pessoa (*told me*); **say** não (*said that*).
- Perguntas relatadas: sem inversão; sim/não com **if / whether**.
- Ordens e pedidos: *tell / ask someone (not) to do*.
- *Enjoy, finish, avoid, mind, suggest* + **-ing**; *want, need, decide, hope, promise, manage* + **to**. Depois de preposição, sempre **-ing**.
- *Stop, remember, forget, try* mudam de sentido conforme a forma.
