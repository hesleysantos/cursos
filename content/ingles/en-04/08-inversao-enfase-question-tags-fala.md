# Inversão, ênfase, question tags e inglês falado natural

Nesta lição você vai aprender a **dar ênfase** em inglês com estruturas de nível B2: a **inversão** depois de expressões negativas ({{en:Never have I seen such a mess.}} / {{en:Not only did we lose money, but we also lost a client.}}), o uso de **so / such** e as **cleft sentences** ({{en:What we need is more time.}} / {{en:It was the auditor who found the error.}}). Vai completar o estudo das **question tags** e, por fim, vai conhecer os recursos do **inglês falado**: fillers, softeners e polidez indireta, aquilo que faz uma pergunta soar natural em vez de seca.

## Inversão depois de advérbios negativos

Quando uma frase **começa** com um advérbio ou expressão de sentido negativo ou restritivo, o **auxiliar vem antes do sujeito**, como numa pergunta. É um recurso formal e enfático, comum em escrita e em discursos.

| Expressão inicial | Frase normal | Frase com inversão |
|---|---|---|
| **Never** | {{en:I have never seen such a mess.}} | {{en:Never have I seen such a mess.}} |
| **Not only... but (also)** | {{en:We not only lost money but also lost a client.}} | {{en:Not only did we lose money, but we also lost a client.}} |
| **Rarely / Seldom** | {{en:We rarely get such good news.}} | {{en:Rarely do we get such good news.}} |
| **Under no circumstances** | {{en:You must not share this under any circumstances.}} | {{en:Under no circumstances must you share this.}} |
| **No sooner... than** | {{en:As soon as I sat down, the phone rang.}} | {{en:No sooner had I sat down than the phone rang.}} |
| **Hardly... when** | {{en:I had hardly started when the system crashed.}} | {{en:Hardly had I started when the system crashed.}} |
| **Only then / Only when** | {{en:I understood the problem only then.}} | {{en:Only then did I understand the problem.}} |
| **Little** | {{en:He didn't know that the audit had begun.}} | {{en:Little did he know that the audit had begun.}} |

A mecânica: se a frase tem auxiliar (*have, will, can, be*), ele passa para a frente; se está no present/past simple, acrescenta-se **do / does / did** e o verbo volta à forma base. {{en:Not only did we lose money}} (e não ~~Not only we lost~~).

> **Dica:** *Not only... but also* é a inversão mais útil para ensaios e apresentações. {{en:Not only is the system expensive, but it is also slow.}} Note que a inversão só ocorre na **primeira** parte.

Com *only when / only after / only if*, a inversão vai na **oração principal**, não na que começa com *only*: {{en:Only when the report was finished did we notice the error.}}

## So e such para ênfase

- **so + adjetivo / advérbio** (+ that): {{en:The deadline was so tight that we worked all weekend.}}
- **such (+ a/an) + (adjetivo) + substantivo** (+ that): {{en:It was such a tight deadline that we worked all weekend.}}
- **so + much / many / few / little**: {{en:There were so many errors that we started again.}}

Também aceitam inversão em registro formal: {{en:So serious was the error that the CFO resigned.}} / {{en:Such was the pressure that two people quit.}}

Erro comum: ~~It was a so tight deadline~~ → {{en:It was such a tight deadline}}; ~~so a good idea~~ → {{en:such a good idea}}.

## Cleft sentences: destacando uma parte da frase

As **cleft sentences** ("frases divididas") reorganizam a frase para colocar o foco em um elemento. Em português, equivalem a "foi X que...", "o que eu quero é...".

### It-cleft: It + be + elemento destacado + who / that

Frase neutra: {{en:The auditor found the error in March.}}

- {{en:It was the auditor who found the error.}} — Foi o auditor que encontrou o erro (não outra pessoa).
- {{en:It was the error that the auditor found.}} — Foi o erro que o auditor encontrou.
- {{en:It was in March that the auditor found the error.}} — Foi em março que o auditor encontrou o erro.

### Wh-cleft: What + oração + be + elemento destacado

- {{en:What we need is more time.}} — O que precisamos é de mais tempo.
- {{en:What I don't understand is why nobody checked.}} — O que não entendo é por que ninguém conferiu.
- {{en:What the client wants is a simple answer.}} — O que o cliente quer é uma resposta simples.

Variantes: {{en:The reason why we lost the client was the delay.}} / {{en:All I want is a clear report.}} (*all* = a única coisa). Na fala, as wh-clefts organizam o pensamento: *What I mean is..., What happened was...*

## Question tags completas

As **question tags** confirmam informação ou buscam concordância. Regra básica: afirmativa → tag negativa; negativa → tag afirmativa; repete-se o **auxiliar** (ou *do/does/did*) e o **pronome**.

| Frase | Tag | Observação |
|---|---|---|
| {{en:You're the new accountant,}} | {{en:aren't you?}} | *be* como verbo principal |
| {{en:She works in finance,}} | {{en:doesn't she?}} | present simple → *does* |
| {{en:They sent the invoice,}} | {{en:didn't they?}} | past simple → *did* |
| {{en:You haven't finished,}} | {{en:have you?}} | negativa → tag afirmativa |
| {{en:He can't come,}} | {{en:can he?}} | modal |
| {{en:I'm late,}} | {{en:aren't I?}} | exceção: não existe *amn't* |
| {{en:Let's start,}} | {{en:shall we?}} | *let's* → *shall we* |
| {{en:Close the door,}} | {{en:will you? / would you?}} | imperativo |
| {{en:Don't forget,}} | {{en:will you?}} | imperativo negativo |
| {{en:There's a problem,}} | {{en:isn't there?}} | *there* repete-se |
| {{en:Nobody called,}} | {{en:did they?}} | *nobody, everyone, someone* → *they*; *nothing* → *it* |
| {{en:You never check,}} | {{en:do you?}} | *never, hardly, rarely* contam como negativos |
| {{en:I think she's right,}} | {{en:isn't she?}} | a tag segue a oração subordinada |

**Entonação**: tag com entonação **descendente** = você tem certeza e só pede confirmação ({{en:It's a good report, isn't it?}}). Entonação **ascendente** = dúvida real, pergunta de verdade.

## Inglês falado: fillers, softeners e polidez indireta

### Fillers (preenchedores)

Dão tempo para pensar sem criar silêncio: *well, you know, I mean, sort of, kind of, actually, basically, the thing is*. Use com moderação; em excesso soam inseguros.

{{en:Well, the thing is, we haven't received the figures yet.}} — Bom, a questão é que ainda não recebemos os números.

### Softeners (suavizadores)

Tiram a dureza de pedidos, críticas e recusas. O inglês profissional é **indireto**: quanto mais formal a situação, mais longa a frase.

| Direto (soa rude) | Suavizado |
|---|---|
| {{en:Send me the file.}} | {{en:Could you possibly send me the file when you have a moment?}} |
| {{en:This is wrong.}} | {{en:I'm not sure this is quite right.}} / {{en:There seems to be a small issue here.}} |
| {{en:No.}} | {{en:I'm afraid that won't be possible.}} / {{en:I'd love to, but...}} |
| {{en:I want a discount.}} | {{en:I was wondering if there might be any flexibility on the price.}} |
| {{en:You're late.}} | {{en:I think we said nine, didn't we?}} |

Recursos típicos: *I'm afraid, I was wondering if, would you mind, it seems, a bit / a little / slightly, perhaps, to be honest, with respect, if you don't mind me saying*. Note o uso do **passado** para suavizar o presente: {{en:I was wondering}}, {{en:I wanted to ask}}, {{en:I thought we could...}}

### Perguntas indiretas

{{en:Where is the report?}} → {{en:Do you know where the report is?}} / {{en:Could you tell me where the report is?}}

{{en:Did he sign it?}} → {{en:Do you happen to know if he signed it?}}

Como no reported speech, a ordem interna é afirmativa (*where the report is*, não ~~where is the report~~).

## Diálogo: negociando um prazo

> **Client:** {{en:So, the report will be ready on Friday, won't it?}}
>
> **Accountant:** {{en:Well, the thing is, we've had a slight problem with the bank statements. I was wondering if Monday might work for you.}}
>
> **Client:** {{en:Monday? That's a bit late, to be honest. The board meets on Tuesday.}}
>
> **Accountant:** {{en:I understand. What I can do is send you a draft on Friday and the final version on Monday morning. Would that help?}}
>
> **Client:** {{en:It would, actually. It's the final figures I really need, isn't it? The rest can wait.}}
>
> **Accountant:** {{en:Exactly. And not only will you have the figures, but I'll also include a short summary for the board.}}
>
> **Client:** {{en:That's very kind. You don't happen to know whether the tax estimate has changed, do you?}}
>
> **Accountant:** {{en:I'm afraid I can't confirm that yet. Only when the bank sends the last statement will we know for sure.}}
>
> **Client:** {{en:Fair enough. Let's talk on Friday, shall we?}}

## Erros comuns de brasileiros

1. ~~Never I have seen~~ → {{en:Never have I seen}}
2. ~~Not only we lost money~~ → {{en:Not only did we lose money}}
3. ~~a so good idea~~ → {{en:such a good idea}}
4. ~~You're coming, no?~~ / ~~isn't it?~~ para tudo → {{en:You're coming, aren't you?}} (a tag depende do verbo)
5. ~~I'm right, am I not?~~ (muito formal) → {{en:I'm right, aren't I?}}
6. ~~Could you tell me where is the report?~~ → {{en:Could you tell me where the report is?}}
7. ~~I want that you send me~~ → {{en:I'd like you to send me}} / {{en:Could you send me}}

## Na prática

Em apresentações e e-mails de ênfase, a inversão e as cleft sentences destacam o que importa (*Not only did revenue grow, but margins also improved*; *What we need is a decision*). Na conversa com clientes, os softeners e as perguntas indiretas separam um pedido educado de um que soa rude (*I was wondering if you could send the statement*).

## Resumo

- **Inversão**: depois de *never, rarely, not only, no sooner, hardly, under no circumstances, only then/when*, o auxiliar vem antes do sujeito (*Never have I..., Not only did we...*).
- **So + adjetivo**; **such (+ a) + substantivo**; ambos com *that* para consequência.
- **Cleft sentences** destacam um elemento: *It was X who/that...*; *What we need is...*; *The reason why... is...*
- **Question tags**: auxiliar oposto + pronome; exceções *aren't I, shall we, will you, nobody → they*. Entonação descendente = confirmação; ascendente = dúvida.
- Fala natural: **fillers** (*well, you know, the thing is*), **softeners** (*I'm afraid, I was wondering if, a bit*), **perguntas indiretas** com ordem afirmativa (*Do you know where it is?*).
